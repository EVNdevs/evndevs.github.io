The story

LEGO® MINDSTORMS® was discontinued in 2022. The material for learning with it thinned out, and serious robotics hardware stayed expensive. EVN is the answer to that: an affordable, open platform — the natural evolution of LEGO MINDSTORMS, and a basecamp for students who want to get serious about robotics.

The board exists because of its backers: the Kickstarter campaign “EVN: The Natural Evolution of the LEGO® MINDSTORMS® System” was funded by 71 backers. Without them EVN would not exist.
